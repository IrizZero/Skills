# Lesson entry

One entry per lesson, in the project's own lessons file. Six lines at most.
No story of the stage: that lives in the stage report.

```markdown
### L<n> - <the rule, as a short sentence> (<date>)

- **What happened:** <the mistake, near miss or failed prediction, concretely>
- **Who:** <PM | builder | tester | tool or app | the method>
- **Cost:** <sessions, owner turns, machine time, tokens, or "not measured">
- **Evidence:** <a number, a file, a commit, the owner's words>
- **Do:** <what to do next time, as an action with a visible sign>
- **General or project:** <general | this project only>
```

Log one when:

- a gate failed, or passed only after a fix;
- the owner corrected the PM;
- a prediction told to the owner turned out wrong;
- a rule of the `pm-style` skill was broken, or did not fit;
- something worked well enough that it should become a habit.
