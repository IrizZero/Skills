# Close-out proposal

Put this to the owner in chat at the end of a project or round.
Change the skill only after the owner's OK, and only the rows approved.

```markdown
## Close-out: <project>, <date>

**Cost:** <PM share of the round, cost by kind of work, share above 300,000 tokens; or "not measured">
**Method:** <stages, owner turns the method caused, gates that caught something; or "not measured">

### Proposed changes to the pm-style skill

| # | Change | Where | Wording | Lesson behind it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Add | `SKILL.md`, hard rules | "<rule>" | L<n>: <what it cost> |
| 2 | Merge | `references/gates-and-tests.md` | old: "<line>" -> new: "<line>" | L<n> |
| 3 | Hook | `hooks/` | reminds when <what it matches> | L<n>, broken <k> times |
| 4 | Drop from the skill | `<file>` | "<line>" | not used in this project, or replaced by row 1 |

**Lessons that stay in the project (not general):** L<a>, L<b>, ...
**Rules already in the skill that were broken anyway:** <rule> - <why it did not hold>
**Size after the change:** `SKILL.md` <n> lines of 200, largest reference <n> of 150.
```
